# Open-Course Hub Governance

## Content Authority

This repository is canonical for institution-neutral open module overviews, common-core navigation, and public offering indexes. The [Lab repository](https://github.com/tulip-lab/agentic-AI-lab/tree/develop) is canonical for public practicals and approved student-facing assignment artefacts.

Restricted teaching material, solutions, marking support, moderation, hidden checks, credentials, student information, and unpublished offering evidence are not public-hub content.

## Status Vocabulary

- **development:** useful work in progress on a development branch.
- **planned:** an offering has been identified but is not yet an active published delivery.
- **approved:** the named bounded content has completed its stated review.
- **released:** a reviewed version has been intentionally published through the repository's release process.

One status never implies another.

## Stable Paths and Assignments

Module IDs, directory names, and session IDs are durable navigation contracts. Approved assignment versions are immutable: corrections use versioned replacements or visible errata rather than silent changes. Offering pages link to canonical Lab artefacts and do not duplicate them.

## Contributions and Validation

Open-module improvements belong in this repository. Practical improvements belong in Lab. Offering or assignment proposals are routed through their accountable owner before public integration. Reviews check links, accessibility, rights, secrets, private boundaries, technical execution, and truthful status claims.

## Approval Boundaries

Creating a candidate does not authorise repository write, merge, push, release, Pages activation, or publication to `main`. Each action requires its own recorded approval. Third-party links retain their source terms; names and marks identify their owners and do not imply endorsement.
